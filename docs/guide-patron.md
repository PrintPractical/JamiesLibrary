# Borrowing books

This guide is for patrons: seeing what you have out and when it is due, renewing, looking back at what you have borrowed, showing your library card, and, where the library allows it, checking books out yourself. To find books, see [Finding books](guide-browsing.md). To get an account, see [Accounts](accounts.md#registering-with-an-invitation-code).

## Your home screen

Sign in and you land on **Home**. At the top it says today's date at the library, a greeting, and how many books you have out, for example `You have 2 books out. One is overdue.` Under it:

- **Your library card**: a link to your card (see [below](#your-library-card)).
- **Search by title, author or ISBN**: opens [Browse](guide-browsing.md).
- **Your books**: every book you have out, with its cover, title and authors, when it is due and what you can do about it. Books that are overdue come first, then the rest by due date. If there are none, it says `Nothing is out on your card right now.`
- **Borrowing history**: a link to everything you have borrowed.
- **Check out a book**: only when the library has switched on self-checkout and you are allowed to use it (see [Checking out yourself](#checking-out-yourself)).

On a phone the same links are in the bar at the bottom: **Home**, **Browse**, **My card**, **My books** (your borrowing history) and **Account**.

## Due dates

Each book in **Your books** has a label that says, in words and not by colour alone:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Due Fri 13 Nov` | The date it should be back. |
| `Due tomorrow` or `Due today`, with the date | It is nearly due. A book due today is still on time all day. |
| `Overdue · 3 days`, with `Was due Fri 13 Nov` | It is past its due date. |

Dates follow the library's own time zone, not your phone's, so a book due on the 13th is on time until the library's day ends.

The due date is set when the book is checked out: the library's loan period (14 days to begin with) after that day.

## Renewing a book

A book that isn't overdue has a **Renew** button and a note such as `Renewed 0 of 2 times`. Press it and the due date moves forward by the loan period *from its current due date*: a book due on Friday with a 14-day period is then due 14 days after that Friday, not 14 days after today. The card updates at once.

Renewing is refused, with the reason on the book, when:

- **it is overdue**: `Overdue books can't be renewed. Please bring it back when you can.` Return it at the desk.
- **it has been renewed as many times as the library allows**: `Renewed the most times allowed (2 of 2), so it can't be renewed again. Please bring it back by Mon 28 Dec.`
- someone returned it in the meantime: `That loan was already returned, so there is nothing to renew.`

If you are not allowed to renew, the library says `You don't have permission to do that.` Ask a librarian, who can renew for you.

## Borrowing history

Press **Borrowing history** (or **My books** on a phone) to see every book you have borrowed, newest first, 50 to a page, with the date you borrowed it and the date you returned it, or `Still out`. Use **Older** and **Newer** to move between pages. If you haven't borrowed anything, it says `You haven't borrowed anything yet.` Only you, and librarians, can see your history.

## Your library card

Open **My card** to see your card: the library's name and logo, your name, a barcode and a QR code, and your 12-digit card number. Hold it up to the desk's scanner or camera; the librarian scans either code. If the scanner has trouble, turn your screen's brightness up. The card is the same every time, and no one else has your number.

If you lose your phone or think someone copied your card, ask a librarian for a new one. They replace the number, and the old code stops working straight away; the books you have out stay yours.

## Checking out yourself

When an administrator has switched on self-checkout, and your account may use it, **Home** has a **Check out a book** box and the page `/checkout` opens. If the switch is off, the page says `Self-checkout is switched off` and `Take the book to a librarian to check it out.`

1. Scan the barcode on the book (with the phone's camera where the page is served securely, or typed in the **Book code** field) and press **Check out**. Libraries identify books in one of two ways: where each copy has its own sticker, scan it; where books are identified by ISBN, scan the ISBN barcode on the book and the library picks a copy of it.
2. A green box says `Checked out: <Title>` with the copy number and the due date, for example `Copy 2, due Mon 30 Nov`. **This visit** lists what you have done on the page, newest first (the last 20). The loan is on your home screen too.
3. Scan the next book, or press **Back to your books**.

A scan can be refused. The box says `Not checked out`, what happened, and the code you scanned:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| `Limit reached: you already have 5 of 5 items out. Return one to a librarian before borrowing another.` | You're at the library's limit. |
| `<Title> is already out. If it's in your hand, give it to a librarian to check in.` | Someone has it on loan, or it was returned without being checked in. Take it to the desk. |
| `No copy of <Title> is available: the only copy is out. Ask a librarian, or try again after a return.` | Every copy is on loan. |
| `<Title> has no copies in the library to lend. Ask a librarian.` | |
| `That is a book's ISBN, and this library identifies copies by their own barcodes. Scan the copy's own barcode.` | Scan the sticker on the book, not the ISBN. |
| `Nothing matches that code. Check the label, or type it again.` | The code isn't one of the library's. |
| `Your account is deactivated, so nothing can be checked out to you. Ask an administrator to reactivate it.` | |
| `Self-checkout is switched off in this library. Take the book to a librarian.` | |

Nothing is lent when you see a refusal.

## Returning books

Books are returned at the desk: a librarian scans them in. You can't check a book in yourself, and a book stays on your list until they do. If you hand a book back but it still shows on your home screen, ask the librarian to check it in.

## If something doesn't work

- **A librarian can't find your card:** the card might have been replaced. Open **My card** for your current number.
- **The camera doesn't start:** see [Scanning](guide-catalog.md#scanning-camera-hand-scanner-or-typing). You can always type the code.
- **`Can't reach the library. Check your connection and try again.`** Your phone has lost its connection to the library. Nothing was changed; reconnect and try again.
- **You can't see Check out, or the history:** each is a permission your administrator can switch off. Ask a librarian or administrator.
